### 2.2 Items 2 and 3: Discover saved, and the slow Aurral playlist page (investigation)

What runs today when Library › Discover or a flow's page opens:

1. **Library › Discover** (`DiscoverListView`, `App/Screens/Discover/DiscoverPlaylistView.swift:239`) shows a spinner
   until `discoverRows()` → `discoverPlaylists()` (`App/AppModel+Discover.swift:322-332`) returns. That waits up to
   `discoverWait` = 3 s (`App/AppModel+DiscoverHome.swift:20`) for a full Aurral read; at launch `discover.playlists`
   is empty, so it then asks Navidrome for **every playlist** (`getPlaylists`) and filters by the saved ids. Nothing
   about the rows (names, owners, item rows, captions) is saved except id sets in UserDefaults.
2. **A flow's page** (`DiscoverPlaylistView.load`, `:228-234`): `if model.discover.playlists.isEmpty { await
   model.refreshDiscover() }` — opened soon after launch (from Home, Recently played, the car) it **waits for the whole
   Discover read with no bound**: `status`, every Navidrome playlist, Aurral's whole `jobs` list (up to 8 MB, D115),
   **each candidate playlist read one after another** (`:244-249`), the item proofs (D251, up to 10 reads), and then
   `refreshAurralLibrary` paging up to 20 × 500 songs per Discover library (`:295-306`). **Likely cause #1** of "takes
   too long", and it grows with the number of flows and songs.
3. Then `discoverPage` (`:365-372`): one `getPlaylist`, then `libraryCopies(for:)` (`:390-433`) looks **every song** up
   that wasn't looked up in the last 30 minutes: by recording MBID through the native API, and when that misses, a
   `search3` of the title, **four at a time** (`:410`). A 100-song flow is up to ~200 requests, and **the page shows
   nothing until the last lookup returns** (`page = .loaded(...)` only after `libraryCopies`). **Likely cause #2**:
   cost proportional to the song count, with the whole wait in front of the user.
4. The lookups live only in memory (`DiscoverState.lookups`, `App/AurralConnectionStore.swift:141`; cleared at `:223`),
   so every launch, and every 30 minutes, the same 200 requests run again. **Likely cause #3.**
5. Songs that need no lookup are looked up anyway: a song in a playlist whose `libraryId` is **not** one of Discover's
   libraries is already the main library's file (Aurral 2.13 files a static playlist's Library-referenced songs as the
   Library's, D251), yet `libraryCopies` asks the server for its "copy". **Likely cause #4** (size depends on how many
   of a flow's songs Aurral referenced from the library: UNCONFIRMED on the owner's server).
6. The car opens a Discover playlist through the same `discoverPage` (`App/CarPlay/CarPlaySceneDelegate.swift:111-113`),
   so the car waits the same way.

Fix proposed (D1): save the Discover rows and each playlist's page (songs and their library copies) per account and
show them at once; refresh in the background and swap in place only when something differs; never wait unbounded for
the Discover read; skip lookups for songs already in the main library; keep lookups across launches; show a page's songs
as soon as the playlist is read and fill library copies in progressively; read the candidate playlists four at a time
instead of one by one. Expected: a page opens instantly from the saved copy; a first-ever open shows songs after one
`getPlaylist`; a re-check sends one `getPlaylist` plus lookups only for songs that are new.

### 2.5 Item 7: Lidarr missing albums and clearing downloads (outside facts verified in Lidarr's source, `develop`)

- **MissingAlbumSearch** (`src/NzbDrone.Core/IndexerSearch/MissingAlbumSearchCommand.cs`): a command with one optional
  `ArtistId`. `AlbumSearchService.Execute(MissingAlbumSearchCommand)`: up to 100,000 albums **without files** where
  `album.Monitored && artist.Monitored`, optionally one artist, **minus albums already in the queue**, searched oldest
  `LastSearchTime` first, one after another inside the command. No release-date filter. So `POST /api/v1/command
  {"name": "MissingAlbumSearch"}` is exactly "search for all missing albums in monitored artists". Lidarr's
  `GET /api/v1/command` lists queued and started commands (Magpie already reads it: `LidarrClient.commands()`).
- **Queue removal**: `DELETE /api/v1/queue/{id}?removeFromClient=true&blocklist=true&skipRedownload=true` removes the
  download from the client, blocklists that release, and doesn't search again by itself (Magpie searches after). Magpie
  already sends exactly this, through the gate, in two confinements: R17's `queueDelete` (only a live Magpie request's
  own download) and D49/D63's **`ConfinedShape.queueRestartAny`** (Settings › Downloads in Lidarr may restart **any**
  download Lidarr grabbed, the owner's too; another app's needs `allowForeign` from a flagged confirmation; never a row
  that is importing, imported or complete, or shares its download with one). The search that follows is the
  `restartSearch` AlbumSearch, only for the albums the removed download named, within 10 minutes
  (`WriteGate.swift:897`). Restart all sends at most **25 per tap** (`DownloadsInLidarr.swift:392`).
- **Commands Magpie may send** (`WriteGate.swift:236-238`): AlbumSearch, ArtistSearch, RefreshArtist, each body exactly
  `{name, <id key>}` (`commandReason`, `:430-445`), and every command is **confined to the ledger**
  (`WriteConfinement.swift:236-256`, Y5 fix I9 and round 12 M2: AlbumSearch only for albums Magpie recorded,
  ArtistSearch only for an artist Magpie created). **A whole-library MissingAlbumSearch conflicts with that
  confinement**: it searches the owner's own monitored albums, not Magpie's. The owner asked for exactly that, so this
  round supersedes the ledger confinement **for this one command shape only**, keeps it behind "Allow Magpie to change
  your library" (G1), and gives it its own named shape and pins (D289–D291).
- **Clearing downloads Magpie didn't start**: removing a download Lidarr grabbed for the owner is **not** a new conflict:
  D49 and D63 (owner decisions, rounds 12 and 13) already superseded R17's "never touches downloads of anything else"
  on the Downloads in Lidarr page, through `queueRestartAny`. Removing a download **another app** started (Lidarr lists
  it but never grabbed it) is allowed today only one at a time behind the flagged confirmation, or by Restart all's
  second button (D63). A "clear everything" that sweeps those in would widen D63 → **owner question 2**.
- Scene hook: `AppModel.scenePhaseChanged` (`App/AppModel+DownloadActivity.swift:42-45`) already runs when the app
  becomes active (R24H resumes monitoring changes there), the right place for "when you open the app".

### 2.7 Items 10 and 11: the error that stays, and transcoded songs that skip (investigation)

What a converted stream is today:

- `NavidromeClient.streamURL` (`:354-364`): `stream?id&maxBitRate&format`, plus `estimateContentLength=true` for
  Original and MP3 only; AAC and Opus go **chunked**, no length, no byte ranges (D42, D45). Default qualities
  (`Preferences.swift:416`): Wi-Fi **Original**, mobile data **AAC 160**: in the car, on mobile data, every song is a
  chunked AAC transcode.
- **Prefetch of the next stream starts at once for every converted song.** `AVQueueEngine.topUp` (`:235-244`) appends
  the next item when the current one has ≤ 20 s left, but reads the length from the item, and a chunked stream's
  length is indefinite, so `remaining = 0` and the next song's stream is opened **as soon as the current song starts**
  (the header comment says so, `:9-10`). Two transcodes and two downloads run side by side for the whole song.
- **Pre-downloads run alongside.** Defaults `preDownload: 3`, `concurrent: 2` (`Preferences.swift:436`): while a song
  streams, the next three are fetched at the same converted quality, two at a time (`AppModel+PreDownload.swift`), and
  D70 may refetch a cached copy too. On mobile data the playing stream shares the link with up to three other
  transfers, and the server runs up to four ffmpeg processes.
- **A dropped connection is taken for a conversion failure.** `Player.handle(.failed)` (`Player.swift:414-445`): for a
  stream that isn't the original, any failure of the current item, the first time, "The server couldn't convert it: play
  the original instead, once" (`:433`) → `restartCurrent(rebuild: true)` reloads the song as the **Original** (on mobile
  data, a FLAC many times the size) and seeks back to the position. A chunked stream can't resume after a dropped
  connection (no ranges), so a cellular hand-over in the car fails the item, and Magpie jumps to a far heavier stream:
  more stalls, and an audible jump while it loads and seeks. This also raises
  `AppModel.transcodeHint` ("Your server couldn't convert this song; playing the original",
  `App/AppModel+Playback.swift:9,30-37`), which **is never cleared by a song change**, only by its ✕ — the owner's
  item 10.
- `Player.lastError` (the "This song didn't play…" line, `NowPlayingView.swift:170-176`) **is** cleared on every new
  entry (`beginEntry`, `Player.swift:623-631`). So item 10's message that "remains onto the next song" is, by the code,
  most likely the hint above; the fix covers both.
- `.stalled` is ignored (`Player.swift:410`); the engine relies on AVPlayer's own waiting.
- A current entry whose URL changes is reloaded from 0 (`AVQueueEngine.setItems`, `:127-147`, the `else` branch). The
  player means to keep the current URL ("the current song keeps playing on the URL it has"); whether every path keeps it
  (a pre-download of the current song landing, D70's swap, a Wi-Fi ↔ mobile change) is **not proven by a test**:
  UNCONFIRMED, pinned by a RED test this round.
- MP3 keeps `estimateContentLength`; if ffmpeg writes more than Navidrome's estimate the server cuts the stream and the
  song ends early (D42 saw it for AAC). Verified for the pinned dev image (0.64.2) only; UNCONFIRMED on the owner's
  version.
- **Navidrome supports `timeOffset` for converted streams**: current Navidrome advertises the OpenSubsonic extension
  **`transcodeOffset`** (`server/subsonic/opensubsonic.go`), which means `stream?…&timeOffset=<seconds>` starts the
  transcode there. Which release first shipped it is UNCONFIRMED; Magpie reads the extensions already
  (`serverAdvertises`).

Likely causes of item 11, most likely first: (1) a network drop mid-song turned into a switch to the Original (jump +
heavier stream), (2) the next song's stream and up to two pre-downloads competing with the playing stream from its
first second, (3) MP3's estimated length cut short (UNCONFIRMED), (4) an unproven path re-sending the current entry with
a new URL (restart from 0; UNCONFIRMED). The settings themselves (AAC 160 on mobile data, Original on Wi-Fi, chunked AAC
and Opus) are appropriate and stay.

### 2.9 Item 14: nd-lyrics (verified: plugin README; Navidrome `core/lyrics/lyrics.go`, `server/subsonic/media_retrieval.go`)

- nd-lyrics is J0R6IT0's **Navidrome Lyrics Plugin** (formerly the LRCLIB plugin), `nd-lyrics.ndp`, Navidrome ≥ 0.63.
  It is a lyrics source in Navidrome's **`LyricsPriority`** setting and "only fetches lyrics when a client requests
  them". It can write sidecar files, cache, and use LRCLIB, KuGou, NetEase and others.
- Navidrome walks `LyricsPriority` in order for each request: `embedded`, a sidecar extension (`.lrc`, `.ttml`…), or a
  plugin name; the first non-empty answer wins. So with e.g. `embedded,.lrc,nd-lyrics`, the plugin is asked **only
  when the file has no lyrics bound or beside it** — the owner's condition, decided by the server.
- The trigger is the ordinary read: **`getLyricsBySongId`** (OpenSubsonic `songLyrics`) calls `lyrics.GetLyrics(mediaFile)`;
  the legacy `getLyrics?artist&title` does the same by name. Both are GETs; no plugin-specific endpoint exists.
- Magpie already calls `getLyricsBySongId` (`NavidromeClient.swift:336-343`), but **only when the Lyrics pane is open**
  (`LyricsContent.swift:179-183`), and the Lyrics button appears only when the server advertises `songLyrics`
  (`NowPlayingView.swift:74`). So with the pane closed, nd-lyrics is never asked. UNCONFIRMED: the owner's
  `LyricsPriority` includes `nd-lyrics` (the owner says it is installed).

### 2.10 Item 15: LRCLIB (verified: lrclib.net/docs)

- `GET https://lrclib.net/api/get?track_name&artist_name[&album_name][&duration]` (duration in seconds; matched within
  ±2 s) → `{id, trackName, artistName, albumName, duration, instrumental, plainLyrics, syncedLyrics, …}`; **404**
  `TrackNotFound` when unknown. No key or account. Ask: identify the client in `User-Agent`; ~90 requests/min per IP for
  an identified client; on **429** honour `Retry-After`.
- Network policy: plan decision 11 (`RefusedMediaHosts.swift:9`) bans **Deezer and Apple** hosts only; LRCLIB is
  neither. ATS allows any https (`App/Info.plist:29-33`), and LRCLIB is https, with no credential (the consent gate is
  for credentials over plain http). But AGENTS.md says "outside media goes through the owner's Aurral server", and a
  direct call sends the song's title, artist and album to a third party from the phone. There is no route on the owner's
  servers that fetches LRCLIB for Magpie except nd-lyrics itself (which already uses LRCLIB server-side). Precedent for
  direct calls: MusicBrainz and ListenBrainz. → **owner question 1**.

### 2.12 Item 17: Automix (verified in Navidrome `core/external/provider_similarsongs.go`, `server/subsonic/browsing.go`)

- **`getSimilarSongs2?id=<song|album|artist>&count=`** (default 50, max 500): asks the agent for similar tracks and maps
  them to **library tracks only** (`matcher.MatchSongs`); for a song with nothing from the agent it falls back to the
  artist and up to 15 similar artists' top songs, weighted, library-matched; for an artist, then a seed mix of the
  artist's own tracks. Agent failures are swallowed. Without a Last.fm agent, older Navidrome returns empty (UNCONFIRMED
  per version). Magpie doesn't call it yet.
- Library-only by construction, one call, no MusicBrainz, no outside host. ListenBrainz and Aurral similarity would
  need matching outside songs to the library (the round 26 machinery) and new routes (Aurral has none allow-listed for
  similar artists); not needed. Fallback inside the library when Navidrome answers too few: the seed artist's own songs
  (`getArtist` + albums) and the seed's genre (`getSongsByGenre`, random offset).
- `Player` has no "replace what's coming up"; `PlayQueue` keeps Play next songs in `upNext` and the rest in `main`.

## 12. Owner questions (recommendation first)

1. **Lyrics from LRCLIB, straight from the phone?** Recommended: yes — with "Find lyrics online" on, the phone asks
   lrclib.net directly (https, no account, sending only the song's title, artist, album and length), only for songs your

   on your server (which can already use LRCLIB itself); the switch would then not be built.

   leave those alone — clear only downloads Lidarr started (removing another app's can break that app and Lidarr can't
   search for them again). Alternative: clear those too, with the confirmation saying how many there are.
3. **"Everyone's flows" on Home.** Recommended: one choice for Home, Library › Discover and the car — the existing "Show
   everyone's flows and playlists", now also shown in Settings › Home & Library — with other people's names under their
   flows. Alternative: Home gets its own separate choice.

   play in full like library songs, songs still downloading say "On its way", and new chart flows start at the whole
   chart (up to 100) so nothing is left as only a preview; existing flows keep their size. Alternative: keep 25 to start,
   and songs below the flow's size keep their previews.
5. **What Automix does to your queue.** Recommended: it replaces what's coming up after the current song (songs you
