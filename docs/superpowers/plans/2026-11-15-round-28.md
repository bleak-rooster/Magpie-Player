### 2.1 Offline mode today (Task 14, D46)

- **Three modes, buried.** `OfflineMode { automatic, on, off }` (`Packages/MagpieKit/Sources/MagpieCore/Preferences.swift:256`),
  default `.automatic` (`:480`). The only control is a picker "Offline mode: Automatic / On / Off" deep in Settings ›
  Downloads & Storage (`App/Screens/Settings/DownloadsSettingsView.swift:60-62`, titles `:185-191`), with no footer
  saying what it does. **GAP: the owner asks for a switch "in settings"; today's control is hard to find and unexplained.**
- **`isOffline`** (`App/AppModel+Offline.swift:53-59`): On → true; Off → false; Automatic → `!serverReachable`
  (`App/AppModel.swift:288-289`, a `didSet` that runs `offlineInputsChanged`). `OfflineBox` (`AppModel+Offline.swift:10-15`)
  lets stream URLs and the scrobbler read it from any context.
- **Manual On sends nothing at all**: `request`/`requestOnce` throw `forcedOfflineError` first (`AppModel.swift:734,753`),
  `performReconnect` returns at once (`:805`), probes stop (`AppModel+Offline.swift:207`); a song still streaming gives
  way to the next one on the phone or pauses with "You're offline" (`:217-220`, `Player.stopUnplayableCurrent`,
  `Packages/MagpieKit/Sources/Playback/Player.swift:618-630`). Persisted (it is a preference), so it survives a relaunch,
  and `init` applies it (`AppModel.swift:422`).
- **Automatic today goes offline after 10 s or 2 failed reconnects** (`ReachabilityState`, `AppModel+Offline.swift:18-38`:
  `offlineAfter = 10`, first probe after `suspectInterval = 5 s`, then every `interval = 30 s`; rule at `:89`). The
  decision is evaluated **only when another failure arrives** (`noteUnreachable`, `:83-94`), so in practice the switch
  happens at the next failure after the window, not at the window. **GAP: owner says 30 s.** The 2-reconnect shortcut
  can switch earlier than 30 s (two probes ~6 s apart on a two-address account). Time is `Date.now` (not injectable),
  so the existing tests (`Tests/MagpieTests/OfflineAppTests.swift:424 threeSecondBlipNeitherGoesOfflineNorSkips`,
  `:439 unreachableLongEnoughGoesOffline`) shorten `offlineAfter` instead of driving a clock.
- **Reconnect probes run only while suspect or offline** (`startProbes`, `:96-119`): a timer loop and a watch on the
  network path coming back (`App/Playback/NetworkMonitor.swift:11-32`, `NWPathMonitor`). Each check is
  `reconnect(rebuildAlways: true)` → `performReconnect` (`AppModel.swift:804-830`): every address in order with
  `GET rest/ping` and **no credentials** (`probe`, `:849-861`, 6 s timeout via `probeTransport`, `:335`), counted only for
  a 2xx real `subsonic-response`; then a login on the address that answered (while suspect a ping isn't enough, D46 B).
  Success → `noteReachable` (`AppModel+Offline.swift:128-142`): probes stop, downloads go again, a failed song is retried,
  queued scrobbles drain. Manual On never probes (`:73,84,180,194`). **This matches the owner's "in the background it
  tries to connect while in auto-offline … if manually set to offline, it doesn't do this check"** — except that iOS
  suspends the app in the background unless it is playing or the car is connected (see §10).
- **What counts as "can't connect"**: only `isConnectionError` (`AppModel.swift:776-781`: timed out, can't connect, host
  not found, connection lost, DNS, not connected, roaming off, can't load) **after** `request`'s failover reconnect also
  failed (`:737-745`). **GAP (edge cases):** a reverse proxy answering **502/503/504** while Navidrome is down, or a
  **captive portal** answering HTML, raises `NavidromeError.http(status:)` / `.unexpectedResponse`
  (`Packages/MagpieKit/Sources/NavidromeKit/SubsonicAuth.swift:29-38`), which are **not** connection errors: no failover,
  no probe, and Automatic **never goes offline** while every page fails. (The probe itself already refuses them.)
- **A successful request while suspect doesn't end suspect** (`request` returns through `noteLocalSuccess`, `:767-771`;
  only a probe clears it). Harmless but slow; an authenticated answer proves more than the ping does.
- **401/403** = the server answers: not offline, the sign-in error shows (D46; `reconnectLogin`, `:146-161`). Kept.
- **Launch with no network**: the saved session is used at once and a reconnect starts (`resumeSavedSession`,
  `AppModel.swift:518-553`); downloads play (pinned: `OfflineAppTests.swift:243 coldLaunchWithNoNetworkPlaysDownloads`).
  With the network up but the server unreachable, today's switch takes one launch reconnect (6 s × addresses) plus a 5 s
  probe. Automatic offline is **not** persisted (`serverReachable` starts `true`).
- **What going offline does** (`offlineInputsChanged`, `:202-226`): `player.isPlayable` = "a file is on the phone"
  (downloads **and** cached pre-downloads), upcoming URLs recomputed, downloads told (`DownloadManager.networkChanged`,
  `App/Downloads/DownloadManager.swift:560-582`: manual downloads wait with "Waiting for a connection", running
  pre-downloads stop and are dropped), a song that failed while suspect moves on to the next song on the phone. Back
  online: scrobbles drain, requests poll, finds/match index/Made for you/Lidarr audit refresh (`restoreAfterOffline`,
  `:229-240`).
- **Notice**: `OfflineNotice` = "You're offline" + `wifi.slash` (`App/Screens/Shared/OfflineNotice.swift:5-14`) on Home,
  Library, Search and Downloads. It doesn't say whether the owner chose it or Magpie did, and offers no way back.
  **GAP.**
- **Other hosts while offline**: there is no single egress gate. Navidrome, Lidarr, ListenBrainz and MusicBrainz share
  `AppModel.transport` (`AppModel.swift:253,399,868,989,1077`); probes use `probeTransport`; LRCLIB
  (`App/AppModel+Lyrics.swift:202`, `LrcLibTransport`), outside art (`App/AppModel+OutsideArt.swift:27`), previews
  (`AppModel.swift:418`), Aurral and slskd have their own. Each feature checks `isOffline` itself (95 files mention it).
  "Offline mode On sends nothing at all" (D46) is therefore true by many separate checks, not by construction.
  **GAP (hardening):** one gate, pinned by a test that counts requests on every transport.

### 2.2 What is kept on the phone today (spec §6 "Offline metadata", Task 14)

- **Per song**: `AudioEntry` + `AudioMetadata` sidecar (`Packages/MagpieKit/Sources/Playback/Storage/AudioEntry.swift:15-72`):
  id, title, artist (**track** artist), artist id, album, album id, track, disc, duration, genre, cover id, suffix, replay
  gain, album cover id (only when downloaded **with** an album), album song count, bit rate/sample rate/depth; quality,
  hashed file name, kind (`download` / `cache`), reasons (`song`, `album`, `playlist`, `favourites`, `artist`, `unknown`),
  dates. Written **once, at download time** (`DownloadManager.swift:239-260`); never refreshed. **No URL, token or
  address** (file names are `sha256(songId|quality)`, `AudioStore.swift:72`). `pending.json` holds metadata, reason and
  quality only (`DownloadManager.swift:28-36`).
- **Per downloaded playlist**: `DownloadedPlaylist {id, name, coverArtId, songIds, savedAt}` (`AudioEntry.swift:75-85`),
  refreshed when the playlist is opened online (`AppModel+Downloads.swift:169-182`: songs added are downloaded, songs taken
  out lose the reason). **No owner**, so another user's playlist offline has no caption.
- **No album or artist records.** Offline pages are **derived from the songs' snapshots** (`OfflineCatalog`,
  `Packages/MagpieKit/Sources/Playback/Storage/OfflineCatalog.swift`): albums grouped by album id (or a local key), album
  artist = **first song's track artist** (`:178-187`), artists grouped by **track** artist id (`:101-107`). **GAPS:**
  (a) a compilation or a featured-artist track lands under the wrong artist and its album shows the first track's
  artist; (b) an **album-artist** page (Library › Artists lists album artists, R11) whose id no downloaded song carries as
  its track artist says "not on this phone" offline (`ArtistView.swift:268-275`); (c) no year, genre or "how many of the
  album's songs" offline; (d) a song renamed on the server keeps its old name on the phone for ever.
- **Covers**: one image per album or playlist downloaded as such (`DownloadManager.fetchCovers`, `:788-817`;
  `AudioStore.saveCover`, `:445-455`). A song downloaded **on its own** saves no cover (`OfflineCatalog.swift:189-195`):
  offline it shows a cover only if the artwork disk cache happens to hold it (`App/AppModel+Artwork.swift:12-41`: memory →
  saved cover → disk cache → server; offline never the server). Covers are removed only with *all* downloads
  (`AudioStore.swift:371-380`); a removed album leaves its cover behind. **GAP.**
- **Where it lives**: `Application Support/Magpie/Audio/<scope>/` (`AudioStore.swift:5-17`), `<scope>` = the account's hash
  (`FindsSetupStore.navidromeScope(base: bases[0], username:)`, `App/AppModel+Finds.swift:12-14`), so failover between
  addresses keeps it and another account or server never sees it. Excluded from backup, readable after first unlock
  (`:503-513`). Sign-out erases the scope (`AudioStore.eraseScope`, `:383`; `AppModel.signOut` → `stopDownloads(erase:
  true)`, `AppModel.swift:680`).
- **Flows**: a Discover page has **no "download to this phone"** action — only per-song (`DiscoverPlaylistView.swift:207`
  `SongDownloadMenuItem`). Offline, `PlaylistRouteView` sends a flow to the ordinary playlist page
  (`DiscoverPlaylistView.swift:7-19`), which finds nothing unless the flow was downloaded as a playlist, which the phone
  can't do. Round 27's saved Discover file (D283) holds every flow's page (song ids, library copies), but is **never read
  offline** (`App/AppModel+DiscoverSnapshot.swift:44`). **GAP: flows don't work offline.**
- **Lyrics**: kept in memory for the session only (D310, D313; `AppModel+Lyrics.swift:221,285,305`). Offline after a
  relaunch: "You're offline". Round 27 parked "saving LRCLIB lyrics on the phone for offline". **GAP** (small).
- **Cached (pre-downloaded) songs** are on the phone too: they play offline when reached through the queue, are never
  listed as downloads, and Search lists them under "Recently played on this phone" (spec §6;
  `OfflineCatalog.recentlyPlayed`, `:159-166`; `SearchView.swift:288-295`). The owner's "only downloaded songs are
  visible" bears on this → **owner question 2**.

### 7.1 The state machine, spelt out (O1 pins every row)

| From | Event | To | Effects |
|---|---|---|---|

| suspect | answered (ping+login, or an authenticated request) | online | cancel probes; downloads go; failed song retried; scrobbles drain |
| suspect | failure | suspect (same since) | — |
| suspect | tick, now ≥ since + 30 s | automaticOffline | go offline (§2.5 effects); probes on the offline schedule |
| suspect | becameActive | suspect(now) | probe now (window restarts) |
| suspect / online | path lost ≥ 2 s (Q1 yes) | automaticOffline | go offline; probe on path return |
| automaticOffline | answered | online | as "suspect → online", plus restoreAfterOffline |
| automaticOffline | pathChanged / becameActive / Try again / pull | automaticOffline | probe now |
| any | authRejected (401/403) | online | sign-in error shows; no offline |
| any (session) | manual on | manualOffline | cancel probes; stop a streaming song; nothing sent |

| any | automatic disabled (Off) | never automaticOffline | suspect still holds manual downloads |
| any | signedOut | online | cancel probes |

## 10. Risks and known limits

1. **iOS suspends Magpie in the background** unless it is playing or the car is connected; then no reconnect check runs
   until Magpie is opened (the owner's "in the background it tries" holds only while iOS lets it). No
   `BGAppRefreshTask` is added for it (a probe there would only find out, not do anything useful). RUNBOOK note.
2. **30 s means a dead tap can wait 30 s** at a café before the phone switches; downloaded songs play throughout and
   pages fall back to the phone's copy (D341), so the wait is only for things not on the phone. Q1 removes the wait when
   there's no connection at all.
3. **A proxy answering errors under load** triggers a probe (no state change unless the ping fails too).
4. **Records only cover pages read online after this build** (and new downloads). Existing downloads get records the
   next time their page opens online; until then they look as they do today.
5. **Playlists you didn't download** appear offline only if Q3 is yes and only once opened online since this build.
6. **Downloaded flows following their flow** (Q4) download new songs under today's rules (Wi-Fi unless allowed on mobile
   data); a weekly flow costs a weekly batch.
7. **Lyrics** are kept only for downloaded songs, and only once shown or read online after this build.
8. **Deleted on the server**: a downloaded song stays on the phone until removed in Downloads (D55); its plays may not
   count.
9. **Covers**: albums downloaded before this build with a single song have none until their page opens online.
10. **Search item 2 cause unconfirmed** (§2.6): the change makes the artist explicit everywhere; Q5 confirms.

## 11. Owner questions (recommendation first)

1. **No connection at all.** When the phone itself has no connection (aeroplane mode, no signal), should Magpie switch to
   your downloads straight away instead of waiting 30 seconds? **Recommended: yes** — nothing can reach your library,
   so waiting only delays your music; the 30 seconds then applies when the phone is connected but your library doesn't
   answer. Alternative: always wait 30 seconds.
2. **Songs Magpie fetched ahead of time** (it keeps a few upcoming songs on the phone while you listen; they aren't
   downloads). Offline, should they be listed? **Recommended: no** — offline, lists and search show only your downloads
   (Search's "Recently played on this phone" goes), but one of those songs already in your queue still plays.
