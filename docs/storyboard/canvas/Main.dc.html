Magpie

Music you own lives in its sleeve. Music you don’t have yet is a bare record.

Like the bird, Magpie spots the shiny things and brings them home. One music app for your own server. Search finds everything. Anything you don’t have yet can be tried in seconds to minutes, kept with one tap, and upgraded to lossless. If you don’t keep it, it fades away. You never see the services behind it.

Sleeve or record

Square art: in your library. Round art with grooves: not yet. Used everywhere, CarPlay included.

Spin-up

While a song is being brought home, its record spins at 33⅓ rpm, with an honest estimate rather than a spinner.

Slip into the sleeve

Tapping Keep morphs the record into a square sleeve. It’s the app’s one bouncy moment.

Fade to keep

Finds lose colour day by day. Keeping one restores it, and the rest leave quietly.

Hallmark
