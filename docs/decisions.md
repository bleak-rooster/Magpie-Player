| D49 | **Settings › Downloads in Lidarr may restart ANY download in Lidarr's queue, not only Magpie's** (owner decision 2026-11-01, plan item 6). It supersedes R17's "never touches downloads of anything else" **for this screen only**; R17's automatic stall handling and Find another copy are unchanged. A top-level Settings row while Lidarr is connected (summary: how many need attention at the last read). The page reads `queue/details` (whole queue, with names) and `downloadclient` every 5 s while open. **Active**: downloading (progress, speed from `sizeleft` between reads, client), waiting to start (`queued`, `delay`), paused, can't reach the download app (`downloadClientUnavailable`), waiting to import, importing; none of the waiting, paused or unreachable ones is ever Stalled or restartable, however long they sit. **Stalled**: a copy that's actually downloading and not moving: Lidarr's own `warning`/stalled status while downloading shows at once (with Lidarr's message), except Soulseek, whose warning is always on (R17); otherwise `sizeleft` unchanged while downloading past the "Give up on a stuck copy after" limit for Soulseek copies, and at least 10 minutes for other clients (that setting was made for Soulseek). The no-progress clock (`QueueStallWatch`, sharing R17's signature) is held by the app per Lidarr, so leaving the page doesn't start it over; it starts when the page first sees a row. **Failed**: a failed download, or a blocked or failed import. Restart = `DELETE /queue/{id}?removeFromClient=true&blocklist=true&skipRedownload=true`, then an AlbumSearch for the albums that download carried, through a new gate shape, `ConfinedShape.queueRestartAny`: the same exact query (any other refused), confined by the gate's own fresh read of the whole queue, refusing a row that is importing, imported or complete, queued, paused, delayed or whose client is unreachable, any row whose download is shared with an importing or complete row, and a row without a download id. Before each restart the row is re-checked on a fresh read by the same rule; one that moved or stopped being stuck or failed is skipped ("It started moving again, so it was left alone"). Restarts go one at a time; Restart all sends at most 25 per tap after one confirmation (which counts the rows it will act on), and stops after two in a row can't reach Lidarr. A download of a live Magpie request's album goes through the runner's `removeCounting` (counted toward its five removals before it's sent; past five it's refused) and the runner's own search. "Allow Magpie to change your library" (G1) still applies, and every write is in the change log. `scripts/check-writes.sh` pins the shape: named only by the page's restarter and the runner's counted removal, only as that exact queue delete. **Accepted residuals:** Lidarr's API has no compare-and-swap, so a row can still move to importing in the milliseconds between the gate's read and the delete. "Restart" is the plan's (owner's) word, a deliberate exception to the glossary's "Find another copy" for this page. The row title names the service, as the owner's plan names the page. | Owner, plan item 6; Y4 + review fix round 1 | Accepted |
